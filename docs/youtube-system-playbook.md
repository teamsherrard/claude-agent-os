# PAGE 2 — WHAT THIS IS

## One system. A whole team's job.
The YouTube System runs everything around the camera:
- **A YouTube strategist** → your Game Plan: audit, 3 pillars, ~50 titles, a 90-day calendar
- **A scriptwriter** → every video written in your voice, ready to read
- **An SEO manager** → titles, descriptions, and tags done the way YouTube actually ranks
- **A community manager** → comments triaged, replies drafted, leads flagged
- **A coach** → Mike's frameworks checking every step, and the honest read when you're stuck

It already knows you — your market, your niche, your voice, your goals — because it reads your Brain.
It never asks you twice. **Your only job is to film.**

**How to use this playbook:** you don't memorize commands. You talk normally, and the right skill fires.
This book exists so you can look up what's possible — and steal the exact phrases when you want them.

# PAGE 3 — GETTING SET UP (once, ~20 minutes total)

## Do this first
1. **Your Brain is set up** (Plugin 1). If Claude doesn't know you, nothing will sound like you.
2. **Install the YouTube System.** The install link is in your course portal.

## Then, in order:

### 1. `"Set up my YouTube system"`
- It reads your Brain and asks **one question**: your channel link, or "starting fresh."
- It builds your folders in Google Drive. No connections, no sign-ins, nothing technical. ~5 minutes.

### 2. Your Game Plan builds automatically — just watch.
One document, saved to your Drive:
- **Channel audit** — real numbers, honest read (works on any channel, old or new)
- **Your 3 pillars** — chosen by lead type and the gap in your market
- **~50 exact video titles** — written the way people search
- **The math** — your business plan turned into videos → leads → deals
- **90-day calendar** — what to film, week by week

### 3. `"Build my content board"` (say yes when it offers)
Your next two weeks land in your own Notion as cards with filming dates. Each card fills itself as you
work — script, SEO, lead magnet, reference videos. Move a date, add your own card, delete one — it
respects all of it. Publish a video and the board adds the next one.

### 4. `"Set up my channel page"` (~15 minutes)
It writes every piece; you paste each one into YouTube Studio: description + about section (with your
brokerage disclosure), links, channel keywords, your playlists, banner text for your design tool, and
upload defaults so every future upload starts pre-optimized. Already have a channel? It reads your
current page and fixes it — keeping what works.

# PAGE 4 — YOUR WEEKLY ROUTINE (this page is the whole system)

## Three sentences a week:
1. `"What should I film?"` → ranked ideas from YOUR plan. Pick one.
2. Open a **NEW CHAT**. `"Make this video"` → everything you need to film.
3. Film it. Then `"Repurpose this"` → a week of content from that one video.

**The one rule: one chat = one video.** Every new video gets a fresh chat. That's how every script, SEO
package, and file stays organized without you filing anything.

**The pace: 2 videos a week is the goal. 1 is the minimum.** Zero is the only way to lose.

## What `"Make this video"` gives you, step by step:
1. **The 3 top videos on your exact topic** — real links, real view counts. Watch them, then beat them.
2. **Title, hook, and 3 thumbnail text options** — locked before writing, so the packaging leads.
3. **The full teleprompter script in your voice** — hook → CTA → value → CTA. Read it, don't write it.
4. **The full SEO package** (next page).
5. **Your lead magnet for this video** — the freebie that turns viewers into leads.
6. **Your board card, filled with all of it** — open one card on filming day and everything's there.

# PAGE 5 — THE SEO ENGINE (automatic — but here's what's inside)

SEO runs by itself as part of "Make this video." You never have to think about it. What lands in every
SEO package:
- **3 title options** — search-first, your city in them, built to beat what currently ranks
- **3 thumbnail text options** — 3–5 words, plus the right image direction for the video type
  (your face for relocation and market updates; the HOUSE and the price for home tours — no face)
- **The description** — your booking link, phone, and free guide in the **first 3 lines**, then the
  keyword summary and timestamped chapters
- **Tags** — a small, market-specific set. (Mike's rule: tags barely matter. Don't overthink them.)
- **Hashtags, a pinned comment** that starts conversations, playlist + end-screen suggestions
- **A "why these" note** — one plain paragraph on what each choice targets, so you learn as you go

**Filmed something on your own, outside the system?** No problem:
`"Do the SEO for this video"` — tell it the topic, it builds the whole package.

# PAGE 6 — AFTER YOU PUBLISH (two commands, every video)

### `"Repurpose this"`
That one video becomes: short-form scripts (ready for Reels/TikTok/Shorts), a blog post, an email to
your database, and social posts. Two videos a week in ≈ ten pieces of content out.

### `"Check my comments"` — then drop screenshots of your comments
It works only on your REAL comments (never invented), and it:
- **Flags the leads first** — *"we're relocating in March, is this area good?"* is a buyer, in public.
  It says so plainly: "2 of these are real leads — answer those today."
- **Drafts every reply in your voice** — you just paste them in YouTube.
- **Skips the trolls** — recommends silence over clapbacks, always.
- **Finds your next videos** — *"6 people asked about property tax — that's your next video, and here
  are the comments proving it."*

Also: `"What are people asking?"` — just the video-idea mining, across your recent comments.

# PAGE 7 — TIMELY CONTENT & COMPETITOR INTELLIGENCE (the unfair advantages)

### `"Something big just happened in my market"`
Rates moved. A big employer announced. A development got approved. Tell it the moment news breaks and
you'll have a timely, search-optimized video idea + script the same day — while every other agent is
still thinking about it.

### `"What's working for competitors?"`
It scans the channels in your market for videos that GENUINELY overperform and hands you your stronger,
localized version of each — better title, better hook, your voice, your communities.

### `"Competition analysis"` (the deeper monthly version)
Finds the proven, durable topics in your market — videos still pulling views 2+ years later — and builds
your recreate list. That's demand that never expires.

### `"Audit this channel: [any YouTube link]"`
Works on ANY public channel — yours, a competitor's, a teammate's. The honest breakdown: what's working,
what's stale, what's missing.

# PAGE 8 — EVERY MONTH (three check-ins, ~30 minutes total)

### `"Market report"`
Your monthly market update video, built to record: the deck you screen-share, with talking points under
every slide. Data sourced and dated — never invented. The highest-leverage video most agents never make;
here it's 20 minutes a month. *(Have the Monthly Market System plugin? It runs your whole month instead.)*

### `"How's my channel doing?"`
Your numbers in plain English — every metric explained the first time, compared against YOUR past videos,
never generic benchmarks. And it tracks past views to what matters: which videos produced comments,
calls, and booked appointments.
- Just share your channel link for the standard read.
- For the deep read (watch time, click-through): drop a screenshot or export from YouTube Studio.

### `"Coach me"`
Mike-style coaching: what worked and why, the ONE highest-leverage fix (with the exact rewrite, not vague
advice), and your next action. Feeling stuck? Say it straight — `"I'm not getting views"` or `"should I
keep going?"` — and you'll get the honest read plus the reframe that matters: a video that booked one
call beats 10,000 views that booked none.

**The habit that powers all of this:** ask every new lead — *"which video did you watch that made you
reach out?"* — and tell the system their answers. That's how it learns which videos make you money.

# PAGE 10 — WHERE EVERYTHING LIVES + THE RULES IT FOLLOWS

## Your files (you never file anything)
- **YouTube Game Plan** — top of your YouTube folder in Google Drive
- **One folder per video** — script, SEO package, lead magnet, repurposing pack, together, named by date
- **Channel Page Kit** — in the Setup folder
- **Your content board** — in your own Notion
- No trackers, no spreadsheets, nothing to maintain. The system reads its own state.

## The rules it follows (why you can trust it)
- **Reads your Brain — never asks twice.** Your market, niche, voice, links: known.
- **Writes in your voice — always.** If your Brain says "never salesy," nothing salesy ships.
- **Fair Housing checked** on every title, script, description, and reply before you see it.
- **Real data only.** It never invents a stat, a view count, or a comment. Every number has a source
  and a date. No real comments in hand? It says so and stops.
- **Drafts only.** It never uploads, posts, or replies AS you. You press every button.
