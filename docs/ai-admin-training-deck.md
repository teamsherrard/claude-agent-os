# Realtor AI Admin — Launch & Training Deck (copy only)

*Paste-ready for Claude Design. 20 slides · 16:9 · brand colours + fonts from the design system ·
one idea per slide, on-screen text stays under ~25 words. "SAY" lines are talking points for the
recording — they never appear on the slide. Quoted phrases in bold are literal things the agent
types or says.*

---

## Slide 1 — Title

**HEADLINE:** Meet Your AI Admin
**SUB:** The Agent Leverage OS — 17 skills. One assistant. Zero training required.
**ON-SCREEN:** It already knows your business. Just talk to it.

SAY: This is the assistant you've been told you need to hire — except it's already inside your
Claude, it already read your Brain, and it starts working the moment you finish this video.

## Slide 2 — The problem it kills

**HEADLINE:** Your Day Is Death by Little Tasks
**BULLETS:**
- Booking, rebooking, "does 2pm still work?"
- Hunting attachments. Chasing replies. Re-confirming showings.
- Remembering what every client said, promised, and needs
**SUB:** None of it sells houses. All of it eats the hours that do.

SAY: Realtors don't burn out on deals — they burn out on the hundred tiny tasks between deals.
Every one of the seventeen skills you're about to see exists to delete one of those tasks.

## Slide 3 — Why it beats hiring

**HEADLINE:** The Explanation Tax Is Gone
**BULLETS:**
- A human assistant needs your business explained — every hire, every time
- Your AI Admin reads your **Brain**: your hours, voice, clients, rules
- Explain your business ONCE. Never again.
**SUB:** It answers in seconds, works at midnight, and never quits.

SAY: The reason most agents still book their own meetings is that explaining the task costs as
much as doing it. The Brain kills that tax — the Admin already knows how you work before you ask
for anything.

## Slide 4 — The safety rules

**HEADLINE:** You Stay in Control. Always.
**BULLETS:**
- **Email is DRAFT-ONLY** — it writes, YOU hit send. Every time.
- Every booking is conflict-checked before it lands
- Every confirmation shows the proof: what · when · who
**SUB:** It does the work. You keep the signature.

SAY: This is the slide that makes everyone relax. The Admin never sends an email on its own —
ever. It drafts in your voice, in your Gmail, and you review and tap send. And you never have to
open your calendar to double-check it, because the proof is in every confirmation line.

## Slide 5 — Get set up (once, ~10 minutes)

**HEADLINE:** Four Steps and You're Live
**BULLETS:**
1. Brain first — Plugin 1 installed, **"Set up my Brain"** done
2. Install **Realtor AI Admin (Plugin 2)** from the same marketplace
3. Say **"Set up my AI Admin"** — it walks you through connecting Gmail, Calendar, Drive
4. Pick your briefing time — it schedules your 7am briefing + weekday inbox sweep

SAY: Setup is a conversation, not a config screen. It checks your connections, points you to
exactly what to click for anything missing, asks the few questions your Brain doesn't already
answer — like when you want your briefing — and installs the two automations that run without
you. Zoom is optional; without it you still get a Meet link on every virtual booking,
automatically. And if you're an Outlook person: everything in this video works the same — the
Admin maps itself to Outlook, OneDrive, and Teams on its own.

## Slide 6 — How to talk to it

**HEADLINE:** No Prompts. No Menus. Just Say It.
**BULLETS:**
- Plain English, exactly how you'd text a great assistant
- One message in → one action → one line back with proof
- Don't know the "right" skill? Doesn't matter — the front door routes it
**ON-SCREEN:** "Book the Hendersons Saturday at 2." — that's the whole skill.

SAY: There is nothing to memorize. Seventeen skills, and the only manual is: say what you want.
If you're ever overwhelmed, say "I'm slammed today" and it proposes the top three things to take
off your plate — you just say go.

## Slide 7 — The daily rhythm

**HEADLINE:** A Day With Your AI Admin
**BULLETS:**
- **7:00am** — briefing arrives: your day, prepped, urgent first
- **8:30am** — inbox swept, labeled, hot-lead replies already drafted
- **All day** — talk to it from the car; it acts while you drive
- **6:00pm** — **"wrap my day"**: log outcomes, load tomorrow

SAY: This is the loop. Two automations run without you — the briefing and the sweep — and
everything else is you talking to it whenever something comes up. The agents who win with this
are the ones who make "wrap my day" a habit; everything else compounds from there.

## Slide 8 — Skill: Morning Briefing

**HEADLINE:** Your Day, Before Your Coffee
**BULLETS:**
- Urgent first — hot leads, today's deadlines, what can't slip
- Every client appointment comes pre-prepped
- Your listings' open houses and closings, surfaced
**ON-SCREEN:** "Run my briefing." · "What's my day look like?"

SAY: It's delivered automatically at your chosen time, but you can call it any moment. The trick
most agents miss: everything you logged yesterday shows up here — the briefing is only as smart
as your wrap the night before.

## Slide 9 — Skill: Inbox + Daily Sweep

**HEADLINE:** An Inbox That Sorts Itself
**BULLETS:**
- Every thread labeled: Hot Lead · Active Client · Under Contract · Lender-Title…
- **Speed-to-lead:** every hot lead gets a reply DRAFTED before you even look
- "NEEDS YOU TODAY" + "WAITING ON" — the whole inbox in ten lines
**ON-SCREEN:** "Sweep my inbox." · "Summarize this thread." · "Draft a reply to Sarah."

SAY: The sweep runs every weekday morning on its own. The killer feature is speed-to-lead — by
the time you open Gmail, the reply to that new lead is already sitting in drafts, in your voice.
Review, send, done — first responder wins the client.

## Slide 10 — Skill: Scheduling

**HEADLINE:** "Book It" Is a Complete Sentence
**BULLETS:**
- Conflict-checked, guests invited, video link attached — automatically
- Reschedules, cancels — one appointment or your whole sick day
- **"Plan my route"** — orders your showings so you're not zigzagging
**ON-SCREEN:** "Book the Lees Tuesday at 4." · "Push my 3pm to Thursday." · "Cancel everything today, I'm sick."

SAY: It knows your durations and buffers from your Brain — a showing books as a showing, a
listing consult books longer, and a virtual meeting gets a video link every single time without
you asking. The sick-day cancel is the one to demo: one sentence clears your whole day — your own
meetings are cancelled for everyone, and for anyone else's meeting the decline note is drafted
and waiting for your send.

## Slide 11 — Skill: Client Memory

**HEADLINE:** It Remembers Everything. Forever.
**BULLETS:**
- Every note, promise, and preference — logged automatically as you work
- Ask about any client, get the full story in seconds
- Promises become follow-ups. Nothing falls through the cracks.
**ON-SCREEN:** "What do I know about the Patels?" · "Remember: the Wus want a legal suite."

SAY: This is the compounding asset. Every conversation you log makes every other skill smarter —
your briefing, your prep, your follow-ups. It's not your CRM and doesn't replace it — it's the
layer your CRM never captures: what clients actually SAID.

## Slide 12 — Skill: Meeting Prep

**HEADLINE:** Never Walk In Cold Again
**BULLETS:**
- Five lines: WHO · LAST · OPEN · WATCH · SAY
- Works for any appointment — by time or by name
- Auto-appears under each client meeting in your briefing
**ON-SCREEN:** "Prep me for my 2pm." · "Prep me for the Hendersons."

SAY: Thirty seconds in the car before you walk in: who they are, where you left off, what's
open, what to be careful about, and one smart thing to say. Clients feel remembered — that's the
whole relationship business, automated.

## Slide 13 — Skill: End-of-Day Wrap

**HEADLINE:** Close Today. Load Tomorrow.
**BULLETS:**
- Say your outcomes in one breath — it logs every one
- Kept promises marked done, leftovers rolled to tomorrow
- Ends with tomorrow's first appointment + your ONE first move

## Slide 14 — Skill: Dispatch (the one everyone talks about)

**HEADLINE:** Talk to It Like It's in the Passenger Seat
**BULLETS:**
- Driving, at a showing, leaving an open house — just talk
- It handles EVERY task in one breath: notes, reminders, drafts, bookings
- Zero questions back. One glanceable report. Nothing ever lost.
**ON-SCREEN:** "Just showed the Andersons 412 Hawkwood — loved it, kitchen concerns. Remind me to send comps Thursday and draft them a thank-you."

SAY: This is the demo moment. That one spoken sentence just logged a showing debrief, set a
dated reminder, AND put a thank-you draft in Gmail — and it reports all three back in one glance.
If it can't figure something out, it never guesses — it parks it and asks in tomorrow's briefing.

## Slide 15 — Skill: Match-Back

**HEADLINE:** "Who Wants This Listing?"
**BULLETS:**
- Names a property → it scans EVERY note you've ever logged
- Ranked top-5 buyers, each with the exact need they told you
- A personal note drafted to each one — never a blast
**ON-SCREEN:** "Just walked a great 4-bed in Cranston — who do I know?"

SAY: This is the payoff for every note you ever logged. No CRM can do this, because the fuel is
what your clients actually said to you. And it's honest — if nobody matches, it says so instead
of inventing interest.

## Slide 16 — Skills: Chase + Tomorrow Confirmations

**HEADLINE:** Stop Waiting. Stop No-Shows.
**BULLETS:**
- **"Who owes me?"** — every unanswered ask, ranked, nudge drafts ready
- Deal-critical first: lenders, sign-backs, condo docs
- **"Confirm tomorrow"** — one confirmation draft per client appointment
**ON-SCREEN:** "Who owes me?" · "Chase the lender." · "Confirm tomorrow."

SAY: Two evening habits worth thousands. "Who owes me" replaces twenty minutes of scrolling sent
mail — and it's polite about it, never nudging the same person twice in two days. "Confirm
tomorrow" is the no-show killer: time, address, what to bring, drafted for every client on
tomorrow's calendar.

## Slide 17 — Skills: Filing + Vendor Dispatch

**HEADLINE:** Find Anything. Book Anyone.
**BULLETS:**
- **"File this"** → attachments land in that client's Drive folder, named right
- **"Pull up the Wus' pre-approval"** → found in seconds, Drive or inbox
- **"Book the photographer for Tuesday at 123 Main"** → request drafted, slot held, confirmation tracked
**ON-SCREEN:** One sentence replaces a lookup, a compose, and a calendar entry.

SAY: Filing is the end of attachment archaeology — every client gets a folder, every document
gets a findable name. Vendor Dispatch reads your trusted-partner list from your Brain: it drafts
the ask, holds the slot, and flags it if the vendor goes quiet.

## Slide 18 — Skills: Feedback Chaser + Friday Scorecard

**HEADLINE:** The Chores You Skip, Handled
**BULLETS:**
- **"Get feedback on 123 Main"** — every showing agent gets a request draft; quiet ones get chased
- Replies summarized by theme, with counts — ready for your seller update
- **"Scorecard"** — your week: what you kept, what's slipping (with fixes drafted), who went quiet
**ON-SCREEN:** "Any feedback yet?" · "What's slipping?" · "Draft them."

SAY: Feedback chasing is the chore every listing agent hates — now it's one phrase, and the
themes flow straight into your seller updates. The Friday Scorecard is the honest mirror: every
slipped follow-up comes with its fix already drafted — say "draft them" and Monday starts clean.

## Slide 19 — Your first week (the test drive)

**HEADLINE:** One Habit a Day, Five Days
**BULLETS:**
- Day 1: **"Set up my AI Admin"** → then **"Run my briefing"**
- Day 2: **"Book [a real client] [day] at [time]"** · **"Sweep my inbox"**
- Day 3: from the car — **"Remember this…"** or a full debrief
- Day 4: **"Prep me for my next meeting"** · **"Who owes me?"**
- Day 5: **"Wrap my day"** → **"Confirm tomorrow"** → **"Scorecard"**

SAY: Don't try to use all seventeen skills on day one. This sequence builds the habit in five
days — one real task per day, each one something you'd otherwise do by hand. By Friday you'll
have a briefing that knows your week and a scorecard that proves what the system caught.

## Slide 20 — What it will never do (+ go)

**HEADLINE:** The Promises
**BULLETS:**
- It will NEVER send an email — you review, you send, every time
- It will never invent a contact, a time, or a fact — it checks or it asks
- Your client data lives in YOUR Drive. Nowhere else.
**SUB:** Your first move: say **"Set up my AI Admin."**
**ON-SCREEN:** Explain your business once. Never again.

SAY: Close on trust. The Admin acts fast because the guardrails are absolute: drafts only, real
data only, your Drive only. Then send them off with the one sentence that starts everything —
"Set up my AI Admin" — and remind them their Brain has to be set up first.
