## Slide — Zapier Can't Find Your Form?

HEADLINE: Zapier Shows "No Options"? You're Just Early.
SUB: Zapier can only list forms that already exist. Do these in order:
BULLETS:
1. Deploy your page first — the form doesn't exist until the site is live
2. Submit your own form once — like a buyer. Then hit Refresh in Zapier
3. Check the account on the Zap step — same account that owns the site
4. List still empty? Click ⋮ → Custom value → type your form's name — works without the list
FOOTER: The order: page live → one test submission → THEN Zapier

SAY: If Zapier says "no options are available" when you're picking your form — relax, nothing is
broken. You're just early. Zapier can only show you forms that already exist, and your form
doesn't exist until two things happen: your page is deployed, and the form has fired at least
once. So deploy the page, then go fill out your own form like a buyer — yes, submit it to
yourself, that's the move — then come back to Zapier and hit Refresh. Boom, there's your form.
If it's still being stubborn, check you're on the right account, and here's the pro move nobody
knows: hit the three dots next to that dropdown, choose Custom value, and just type your form's
name in. The Zap works fine without the dropdown ever loading. Remember the order and you'll
never see this screen again: page live, one test submission, then Zapier.
