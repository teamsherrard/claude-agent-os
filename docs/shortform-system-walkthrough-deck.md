## Slide 2 — The real bottleneck
You already know short-form grows your business. What stops you isn't filming — it's everything *before* it:
- Staring at a blank screen: "what do I even post?"
- Writing scripts that don't sound like you
- Re-formatting the same idea for IG vs TikTok vs Shorts
- Falling off after week two
**This system removes all four.**

---

## Slide 3 — What it is
An always-on short-form **operating system**. You think in 3 simple formats; Claude does the thinking for each — the idea, the hook, the script, the captions — all in your voice, pulled from your Brain.
- **Text only:** it scripts and packages; you record and post
- It never films or designs (your design tool does that)
- It quietly keeps your content funnel balanced so you don't have to

---

## Slide 4 — How it works (the loop)
1. **Your Brain knows you** — market, niche, voice, offer, avatars
2. **You pick a format** (or just ask for ideas)
3. **Claude writes it** — hook + script/talking points + per-platform captions & hashtags
4. **You record, approve, post** — through your own scheduler
5. **It reads your numbers** and gets smarter for next time
> One recording session → a week of content.

## Slide 5 — The 3 formats (the only thing you think about)
- 🟢 **Green Screen** — react to what's happening right now
- 🎤 **Talking Head** — batchable value, straight to camera
- 🖼️ **Carousels** — the no-film lane

Behind the scenes, Claude balances the **4-3-2-1 mix** (reach / value / trust / conversion) so your feed pulls in *leads*, not just views — you never have to track it.

---

## Slide 6 — Format 1: Green Screen (your reach engine)
React to what's happening in your market this week.
- Claude finds a fresh local article, news item, or "things to do" list
- You get: a bold **hook + talking points + the article link** to stand in front of
- ~30–60 seconds — point, react, read the hook
- Built to pull in **locals** (buyers & sellers), not other agents
> The fastest content you'll make.

---

## Slide 7 — Format 2: Talking Head (batch a month in a morning)
Your value content — you, teaching one useful thing.
- A topic list on the **80/20 rule**: 80% broad reach, 20% niche
- Pick the ones you like → full scripts **in your voice** (teleprompter + riff-from bullets)
- 45–60 second sweet spot; film 5–10 in one sitting
- Each one comes packaged for every platform
> The batchable engine that keeps you consistent.

---

## Slide 8 — Format 3: Carousels (the no-film lane)
For the days you don't want to be on camera.
- Claude writes the cover hook + every slide, slide by slide
- Plus the **design direction** — you drop it into Claude Design to build
- Great for **saves and shares** ("send this to someone who's moving")
> No filming, still growing.

---

## Slide 9 — The Platform Optimizer (the multiplier under everything)
One idea → native versions for every app, automatically:
- **Instagram + Facebook** — caption + 3–5 searchable hashtags
- **TikTok** — one-line keyword caption
- **YouTube Shorts** — search title + description + tags
- **+ cover/thumbnail text + on-screen cues** for video
> Same idea, packaged to win on each app — never one caption pasted three times.

---

## Slide 10 — What actually makes short-form work (baked into every script)
Mike's framework, in every single piece:
- **HOOK** — ~3 seconds, bold, stops the scroll (never "stop scrolling")
- **VALUE** — delivered as a clean list ("3 things…")
- **CTA** — doesn't always sell: follow / save / send / DM a keyword
Plus: 3–5 hashtags max · captions on every video · one idea per video.
> You don't memorize this — it's in every draft.
