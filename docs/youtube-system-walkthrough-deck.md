## SLIDE 7 — The weekly rhythm (this is the whole job)

**ON SLIDE:**
## Your week, forever:
1. `"What should I film?"` → ranked ideas from YOUR plan
2. Pick one → open a **new chat** → `"Make this video"`
3. Film it → `"Repurpose this"`
**One chat = one video.** Everything for that video lives in its chat.

**SAY:** Memorize this slide and you know the whole system. New video, new chat — that keeps every video's
script, SEO, and files together and organized automatically.

**ON SLIDE:**

## SLIDE 9 — Then you film. That's it.

**ON SLIDE:**
# Your only job: press record
- Phone + a $20 lav mic — that's the studio
- Read the script. One take. Move on.
- 10–25 minutes, hook first, never "welcome back" — **the script already did this for you**
### Done is better than perfect.

**SAY:** The system deliberately leaves you one job — being on camera. Don't add jobs back. A good video
you shipped beats a perfect one you didn't.

## SLIDE 10 — After you publish

**ON SLIDE:**
## Two commands after every video
- `"Repurpose this"` → shorts scripts, a blog post, an email, social posts — one video becomes a week of content
- `"Check my comments"` → your REAL comments, triaged:
  🔥 **leads flagged** — "2 of these are buyers, answer today"
  ❓ replies drafted in your voice — you just paste
  💡 next video ideas from what viewers literally asked

**SAY:** The comments one is sneaky-powerful. People type "we're moving in March, which neighborhood?"
under your videos — those are leads sitting in public. The system finds them, drafts the reply, and tells
you which ones matter today.

## SLIDE 11 — Every month (three check-ins)

**ON SLIDE:**

- `"How's my channel doing?"` → plain-English analytics, tracked down to **leads**, not views

**SAY:** The market update is the highest-leverage video most agents never make — here it's twenty minutes

that matters, the way I would.
