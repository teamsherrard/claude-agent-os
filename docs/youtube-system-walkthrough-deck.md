## SLIDE 8 — Step 3: Build your content board

**ON SLIDE:**
# Step 3
## Type: `"Build my content board"`
Your plan becomes a board in your own Notion:
- Your next two weeks of videos, as cards with filming dates
- Each card fills itself as you work: script, SEO, lead magnet, reference videos
- Move a date, add your own card, delete a card — it respects all of it
- When you publish a video, the board adds the next one

**SAY:** This is the same board I run my own channel on. On filming day you open one card and everything
is on it. And it's two-way — if you move a filming day to Thursday, the system plans around Thursday.

## SLIDE 10 — Your weekly routine

**ON SLIDE:**
## Your weekly routine
1. Type `"What should I film?"` → you get ranked ideas from your plan
2. Pick one. Open a **new chat**. Type `"Make this video"`
3. Film it. Then type `"Repurpose this"`
**One chat = one video.** Everything for that video stays in its chat.
**2 videos a week is the goal. 1 is the minimum.**

**SAY:** This slide is the whole system. New video, new chat — that keeps every video's files together
automatically. Two a week builds the channel. One a week still works. Zero is the only way to lose.

## SLIDE 13 — Then you film

**ON SLIDE:**
# Then you film

- Read the script. One take. Move on.
- 10–25 minutes. Hook first. Never "welcome back." The script already handles this.
### Done is better than perfect.

**SAY:** The system leaves you one job — being on camera. Don't add jobs back. A good video you shipped

## SLIDE 14 — Batch filming days

**ON SLIDE:**
## Type: `"Plan my filming day"`
- Lines up your next 3–4 scripted videos
- Gives you the filming order — same outfit grouped, fewest setup changes, intros back-to-back
- Puts a Filming Day block on your calendar if you want
Also: `"Am I on track?"` · `"Plan my month"` · ask for the **Monday email** if you want one

**SAY:** This is how busy agents do it — film four videos in one afternoon, and the rest of the week is
just uploading. The system gives you the filming order so you're not changing shirts six times.

## SLIDE 15 — After you publish

**ON SLIDE:**
## After every video, two commands
- `"Repurpose this"` → shorts scripts, a blog post, an email, social posts
- `"Check my comments"` → drop screenshots of your comments, and it:
  - flags the **leads** — "2 of these are buyers, answer today"
  - drafts the replies in your voice — you paste them
  - pulls video ideas from what viewers asked

**SAY:** Pay attention to comments. People type "we're moving in March, which neighborhood?" under your
videos — those are leads sitting in public. The system finds them, drafts the reply, and tells you which
ones matter today.

## SLIDE 16 — Every month

**ON SLIDE:**
## Every month, three commands
- `"Market report"` → your market update deck + talking points. Screen-record it.
  *(Have the Monthly Market System? It runs your whole month instead.)*
- `"Run my deep dive"` → the monthly report: your channel, your competitors, the gaps, your next 30 days
  *(first time it offers the live data sign-in — one click, once; or drop a Studio screenshot)*
- `"How's my channel doing?"` → the quick read anytime, in plain English, tracked to leads — not views
- `"Coach me"` → what worked, the ONE fix, your next action

**SAY:** And build this habit: ask every new lead — "which video did you watch that made you reach out?"
Tell the system their answers. That's how it learns which videos make you money, and that's what the
coach works from.
