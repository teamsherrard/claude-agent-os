## SLIDE 2 — What it is

**LAYOUT:** Left: one big definition. Right: a three-line "what it's NOT" list.

**ON SLIDE:**
## The connection that lets Claude read your real numbers
Composio is a connector you add to Claude once. After that, Claude can read your real YouTube and
Instagram numbers — the same ones you see in YouTube Studio and Instagram Insights — whenever you ask
for a deep dive.
**It is not:**
- Not a dashboard you open. You never look at Composio.
- Not another app you post from. It reads. It never posts, replies, or changes anything.
- Not part of setup. It only comes up the first time you run a deep dive.

**SAY:** Think of it as plumbing. You install it once, and from then on your numbers flow to Claude when
you ask. You never open it, you never log into it again, and it can't touch your channel — it only reads.

## SLIDE 3 — How it works (the MCP part, in plain words)

**LAYOUT:** A simple four-box flow left to right: You → Claude → Composio → YouTube / Instagram, with
"read only" and "with your permission" as small labels on the arrows.

**ON SLIDE:**
## The plug, the bridge, the numbers
- **MCP** is the standard plug that lets Claude use outside tools. Every "connector" in your Claude
  settings is an MCP plug.
- **Composio** is one plug with 500+ apps behind it. We use two: YouTube and Instagram.
- **When you say "run my deep dive":** Claude asks Composio → Composio asks YouTube or Instagram, using
  the sign-in you gave it → the numbers come back → Claude reads them like a coach.
- **Your passwords never go through Claude.** You log into Google and Instagram on their own pages.
  Composio holds the key. You can revoke it any time in your Google or Instagram settings.

**SAY:** MCP is just the name of the plug standard. Composio is one plug that happens to carry five
hundred apps. When you ask for your deep dive, Claude sends the question through Composio, Composio asks
YouTube on your behalf, and the numbers come back. Your password never touches Claude — you sign in on
Google's own page, same as any app.

## SLIDE 5 — Connect Composio to Claude (one time, 2 minutes)

**LAYOUT:** Five numbered steps, big. The URL in a copyable box.

**ON SLIDE:**
## Add the connector
1. In Claude, click **Customize** → **Connectors**
2. Click the **+** → **Add custom connector**
3. Name: **Composio**
4. URL: **https://connect.composio.dev/mcp**
5. Click **Connect**. A browser window opens — sign in (or create your free Composio account). Done.
Same steps in Claude on the web, the desktop app, and Cowork.

**SAY:** **DEMO** — screen-share Claude: Customize, Connectors, plus, Add custom connector, type
Composio, paste the URL, Connect. The browser opens, you sign in with Google, close it. That's the entire
install. You will never do this again.

## SLIDE 8 — The depth: what it reads

**LAYOUT:** Two columns (YouTube · Instagram), then one honest row underneath ("what it can't see").

**ON SLIDE:**
## What comes back
**YouTube (live):** subscribers and growth · every video's views, likes, comments, length, publish time ·
each video's title, description, tags and captions (a titles-and-thumbnails check) · your playlists and
channel page · your competitors' channels, every video · where you rank for any phrase.
**YouTube (your Studio pack — four screenshots, once):** click-through rate · watch time and where people
leave · how people found you and the exact search terms · who's watching and when. YouTube keeps these
private — no connection can read them.
**Instagram (live):** every post's views, reach, saves, shares, comments · reels: watch time and skip
rate · when your followers are online · link taps and profile actions · who follows you, who saw you, who
reacted — by city and age · stories live today · your comments.
**Plus:** an AI answer engine — do you show up when someone asks the buyer's question?
**What it can't see:** other people's Instagram or TikTok numbers (a glance at their public profile), and
YouTube comments on some sign-ins (screenshots cover it).

**SAY:** This is real depth. On Instagram it reads the numbers no scheduler shows you: how long people
watched your reel, how many swiped away in the first three seconds, when your own followers are online,
and which posts made someone tap your link or call. On YouTube it reads every public number and checks
every title and description — and for the four private numbers only Studio has, it asks you for four
screenshots, once. And we're honest about the edges: nobody gets other people's Instagram numbers, so
competitors there are a glance.

## SLIDE 9 — What it powers: the deep dive, and the cool stuff

**LAYOUT:** Left: the four parts of the report. Right: six short "cool things" bullets.

**ON SLIDE:**
## One command, once a month — and it all comes from this connection
**The report:** your channel or account (every post ranked, best hooks quoted, the funnel leak) →
your competitors → the gaps → your next 30 days, with the one move. Saved to your Drive, stamped.
**The cool part:**
- It judges you against your own numbers — never a made-up industry average
- It tells you which hook style holds and which loses people in 3 seconds
- It finds the topic your market searches that no local agent owns
- It remembers last month, so month 2 shows you what moved
- It writes what it learns into your Brain — every script after that gets smarter
- Quick questions any time: "how did this post do?" gets a straight answer, not a report

**SAY:** Everything on this slide exists because of the connection. Without it you'd be reading
screenshots. With it, every month you get a coach reading your real board — what worked, why, what to
film, and the one thing to do this month. And it compounds: the second dive knows the first.
