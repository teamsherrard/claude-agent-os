## SLIDE 2 — What it is

**ON SLIDE:**
## One command. One monthly report.
Type: `"Run my deep dive"`
You get a full breakdown of:
1. **Your channel** — every video ranked, what's pulling, what's leaking
2. **Your competitors** — what they do that you don't
3. **The gaps** — topics your market is searching that nobody local owns
4. **Your next 30 days** — 8 exact titles, ready to film
Saved to your Drive. Done once a month.

**SAY:** This is not a dashboard. A dashboard shows you numbers and leaves you to figure out what they
mean. This reads your numbers like a coach, tells you why, and hands you the plan.

---

## SLIDE 3 — How it works: the live data connection

**ON SLIDE:**
## Where the numbers come from
- **Composio** is the connection that lets Claude read your real YouTube data — the same numbers you see
  in YouTube Studio
- **One sign-in, one time.** The first time you run your deep dive, it asks: *"want me to hook into live
  YouTube data?"* Say yes, a sign-in box opens, you log into YouTube. Done forever.
- **Read-only.** It can look at your channel. It can never post, edit, or change anything.
- **Never during setup.** It only comes up here, when you actually run the analysis.

**SAY:** You never open Composio. You never see a dashboard. It's plumbing — you say yes once, and from
then on the deep dive pulls your real numbers automatically. And it's read-only: nothing on your channel
ever changes without your hands on the keyboard.

## SLIDE 4 — The data it gives you

**ON SLIDE:**
## What it reads
**From the live connection (automatic):**
- Subscribers and growth since your last dive
- Every video: views, likes, length, publish date
- Your competitors' channels — every video, in full
- What's ranking in your market for any topic

**From your YouTube Studio screenshot (drop it in when asked):**
- Click-through rate (how often people who see your thumbnail click)
- Average view duration and % viewed (how long they stay)
- Traffic sources and the exact search terms that found you

**SAY:** The live connection covers everything public. The private numbers — click-through, watch
time, search terms — only exist inside your YouTube Studio, so for the full read you drop in one
screenshot. That's the deepest data the deep dive uses, and it's what settles the biggest question:
is it the thumbnail, or is it the video?

## SLIDE 5 — How to use it

**ON SLIDE:**
## The monthly routine (about 10 minutes)
1. Type `"Run my deep dive"`
2. First time only: say yes to the live data sign-in
3. When it asks, drop a screenshot of YouTube Studio → Analytics → Content
4. Read the report. Start with the 3-sentence verdict.
5. Say `"start the first video"` — and go film
Any time between dives: `"How's my channel doing?"` or `"How did my last video do?"` — the quick read.

**SAY:** That's the whole process. The first run has the one-time sign-in; every run after that, you
type the command, drop the screenshot, and read. Ten minutes a month.

## SLIDE 6 — Part 1: Your channel

**ON SLIDE:**
## What it finds in your own videos
- **Every video ranked** by pillar and content type — which lane is pulling, which is dead weight
- **Packaging read** — which titles and thumbnails earn the click, and which 3 to re-title
- **Hook vs. middle** — did they leave in the first minute (the hook) or drift off later (the pacing)?
- **Your 3 best openings, quoted word for word** — and why each one held
- **The funnel leak** — the single biggest spot where views stop turning into calls
- **Cadence** — videos per week vs. your plan, and which pillar you're starving

**SAY:** The funnel leak is the line most agents have never seen. Views → clicks → watch time → the
call-to-action → booked calls. The report names the one place it breaks — like "strong views on your
tours, zero calls, because your CTA is at the very end." That's a fix, not a feeling.

---

## SLIDE 7 — Parts 2 & 3: Your competitors and the gaps

**ON SLIDE:**
## Competitors
- 3 to 5 channels in your market — every video pulled
- **Their outliers** — videos doing 3×, 5×, 10× their normal. A small local channel over-performing counts double.
- **What they do that you don't** — and what you do better
## The gaps
- Topics your market searches where the top result is old, weak, or not local
- **The starving pillar** — what your plan said to film that you haven't
- **Your own winners, from new angles** — the videos worth 2–3 more versions

**SAY:** Outliers are the honest read on competitors. Not "they have more subscribers" — it's which
specific videos over-performed for them, because that's proof a topic works in your market. Then the
gaps: the demand is real, and nobody local owns it. That's where your next videos come from.

---

## SLIDE 8 — Part 4: Your next 30 days

**ON SLIDE:**
## The plan, ready to film
- **Double down** — your 3 strengths, each with the number that proves it
- **Fix** — your 3 fixes, each with the specific change (a rewritten title, a moved CTA — not "improve your hooks")
- **The plan** — 8 exact titles for the month, week by week, on Mike's content mix
- **The one move** — the single most important thing this month
Then: `"start the first video"` → it opens the full "make this video" flow.

**SAY:** Every deep dive ends in action. Not advice — titles. Eight of them, written the way people
search, balanced across the content types, seeded from what already worked for you and the gaps we
found. And the report closes with one sentence: the one thing to do this month.

## SLIDE 9 — The power behind it

**ON SLIDE:**
## Why this beats any dashboard
- **It knows what each video WAS** — pillar, type, hook — so it talks in your language, not video IDs
- **It diagnoses in order** — is it the topic, the packaging, the hook, the middle, or the CTA? The first weak link is the cause.
- **Your own numbers are the benchmark** — your median, never a made-up industry number
- **Mike's frameworks are the judge** — the content mix, the title rules, leads over views
- **It teaches the whole system** — the findings are saved to your Brain, so "what should I film?" and your coach get smarter every month
- **It never invents a number.** Thin data is called thin.

**SAY:** This is the part I'm proudest of. When it says your market updates get shown but not clicked,
that's read straight from your click-through rate against your own median. When it says the hook lost
them, that's the retention data. And every finding writes back into your Brain — so next month, every
other part of the system already knows what worked.
