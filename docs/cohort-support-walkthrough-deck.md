## Slide 5 — How it works

**HEADLINE:** Four Moves, Always in Order
**BULLETS:**
1. **Answers** instantly — the stuff it knows cold
2. **Checks** your actual setup — never guesses
3. **Fixes** by starting the right skill for you
4. **Escalates** to a human — with the ticket pre-written

SAY: Walk through the four moves — most questions die at step one or two. And notice what's NOT
here: it never shrugs, and it never sends you off to go figure something out alone.

## Slide 6 — Why it's different #1

**HEADLINE:** It Doesn't Guess. It Looks.
**BULLETS:**
- Actually checks your Brain — there? complete? backed up?
- Tests your Google connection with a real read
- Reads your screenshots — show it, don't describe it
**SUB:** Diagnosis by evidence, not vibes.

SAY: This is the big one. Say "my brain isn't working" and it doesn't recite tips — it goes and
LOOKS at your brain, your sync, your connections, and tells you what it found. And screenshots:
don't struggle to describe an error. Screenshot it, drop it in. It reads them.

## Slide 7 — Why it's different #2

**HEADLINE:** Always This Week's Answers
**BULLETS:**
- Pricing, plans, limits — pulled LIVE from official pages
- Checks Anthropic's status page when things feel broken
- Reads Claude's update notes weekly, so nothing surprises you

SAY: Claude ships updates constantly. This thing never answers money questions from an old
screenshot — it opens the official page in the moment. And when Claude itself is having a bad day,
it checks the status page first and tells you "not you, not your setup, try again in an hour."

## Slide 8 — Why it's safe

**HEADLINE:** It Never Touches Your Stuff
**BULLETS:**
- Support is read-only — looks, never edits
- Your Brain stays yours — your files, your own cloud drive
- Nothing ever auto-sends — you approve everything

SAY: The support desk is a diagnostician, not a surgeon. When something needs fixing, it starts
the proper skill and you watch it happen with your approvals — support itself never operates on
your data. Built that way on purpose.

## Slide 9 — How to talk to it

**HEADLINE:** Talk Like a Human. It Translates.
**BULLETS:**
- Plain English — "the thing with my emails is broken"
- No tech words needed, ever
- Phone + voice mode: literally SAY "help" while driving
**SUB:** Screenshots beat descriptions. Always welcome.

SAY: You don't need the right vocabulary — "the email thingy stopped" is a perfectly good bug

an assistant.

## Slide 10 — When to use it: setup moments

**HEADLINE:** Ask It: Getting Set Up
**BULLETS:**
- "Am I set up right?" → full checkup, resumes at the first gap
- "Connect my Gmail" / "wrong Google account"
- "Which plan do I need?" → today's real prices

## Slide 11 — When to use it: mid-work moments

**HEADLINE:** Ask It: Something Broke
**BULLETS:**
- "My brain isn't working" / "it forgot everything"
- "I typed it and nothing happened"
- "My edit stopped halfway"
- "Claude is slow today" / "I hit my limit — now what?"

SAY: These are the 9pm panics — and every one of these has a known cause and a known fix. The
"it forgot everything" one especially: your Brain lives in YOUR own cloud drive; I've yet to see
real data loss. It'll prove that to you in about a minute.

## Slide 12 — When to use it: understanding moments

**HEADLINE:** Ask It: "Wait, How Does This Work?"
**BULLETS:**
- "Chat vs Cowork — when do I use which?"
- "Why did my chat disappear?" (spoiler: nothing's lost)
- "What are these Allow boxes?"
- "Is my client data private?"

SAY: No dumb questions — these exact four are half the cohort's questions, which is why it answers
them perfectly. Ask the "embarrassing" basics here instead of sitting on them; that's literally
what it's for.

---

## Slide 13 — What NOT to ask it

**HEADLINE:** Wrong Door (It'll Point You Right)
**BULLETS:**
- Real estate or legal advice → your broker, your lawyer
- "Write my listing / make my reel" → your system skills do that
- Passwords or card numbers → NEVER, in any chat
- Billing problems → it routes you to Anthropic's official desk

SAY: Support answers questions about the system — the system itself does your work. Ask it to
write a listing and it'll happily hand you to the right skill, so wrong door still gets you there.
And hard rule: nothing — not even support — ever needs your password or card number in a chat.

## Slide 14 — When it can't fix it

**HEADLINE:** You'll Never Write a Support Ticket
**BULLETS:**
- It builds the full ticket FOR you — error, screenshot, everything tried
- You paste it into the support portal — one click, submitted
- You never explain the problem twice

SAY: When something needs my team, here's the promise: it packages everything from your session
into a complete ticket, you paste it into our support portal, hit submit. My team gets a perfect
ticket, you get your evening back — and every report makes the system better for the whole
cohort.
