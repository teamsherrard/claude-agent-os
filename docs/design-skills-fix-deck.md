## Slide 2 — How it actually works

**HEADLINE:** One Account. One Skill Drawer.
**BULLETS:**
- Your Claude account has ONE skill list — think of it as the "drawer"
- The drawer lives at: **claude.ai/customize/skills**
- Claude Design shows **whatever is in that drawer**
**SUB:** Design can't see it = it's not in the drawer Design is looking at.

SAY: Here's the whole mental model in one line: your account has one skill drawer — that page
right there — and Design reads that drawer. So if Design can't see your skill, the skill isn't
in the drawer Design is looking at. There are only three ways that happens, and that's our
three checks.

## Slide 3 — Check #1

**HEADLINE:** Same Account. Both Places.
**BULLETS:**
- Check the account email/initials in the corner of **Design**
- Now the corner of your **regular Claude**
- They must be the **EXACT same account**
- Work in two accounts? **Upload the zip in BOTH** — drawers don't share
**SUB:** Skills never transfer between accounts. Team + personal = two separate drawers.

SAY: Check one — and it's the #1 cause. Look at the corner of both screens: same account? If
you have a team account AND a personal one, hear me — those are two different drawers, and a
skill uploaded on one will never show on the other. Signed into the wrong one? Switch, and you
might be done right here. And if you genuinely work in both: upload the zip in both accounts.
Thirty seconds, and this never bites you again.

## Slide 4 — Check #2

**HEADLINE:** Is It Actually in the Drawer?
**BULLETS:**
- Open **claude.ai/customize/skills**
- Skill listed, toggle **ON**? ✅ Go to check 3
- Not there? Upload it: **+ → Upload a skill → the ZIP**
**SUB:** ⚠️ Installing plugins ≠ uploading skills. The course zips go HERE.

SAY: Check two: open the drawer and look. If the skill is listed with the toggle on, you're
good — go to check three. If it's NOT there, that's your whole problem: the zip never landed.
And here's the trap half of you are in — installing my plugins does not put skills in this
drawer. Plugins and skills are different things. The course skill zips get uploaded on this
exact page. Upload it, and it shows up in Design immediately.

## Slide 5 — Check #3

**HEADLINE:** Refresh It — Properly.
**BULLETS:**
- Close Design completely, reopen — filter box **empty**
- **Log fully OUT of claude.ai, log back in** (refreshes the account, not just the page)
- Try Design in a **private/incognito window** — shows there? It was your browser, not you
- Still gone after ALL that? Screenshot both screens → support ticket
**SUB:** Those 2 screenshots tell us exactly what to flag.

SAY: Check three, the refresh ladder: close Design all the way and reopen, and make sure that
little filter box is empty — it hides skills if there's text in it. Still missing? Log all the
way out of claude.ai and back in — that refreshes your account, not just the page. And here's a
pro tester's trick: open Design in a private window. If the skill shows THERE, your browser was
holding onto old information — nothing was ever wrong with your account. And if it's still gone
after all of that, you've found something real: screenshot your skill drawer and Design's
panel, drop both in a ticket, and we take it from there. You did everything right.

## Slide 6 — The two backups (never be stuck)

**HEADLINE:** Can't-Fail Backups — Use Them Today
**BULLETS:**
- **Backup 1:** attach the skill **ZIP right into your Design chat** (+ → the zip → "use this
  skill for this design") — Claude reads the file, no panel needed
- **Backup 2:** run the skill in a **regular chat**, then paste/attach what it produced into
  Design
**SUB:** The skill does the thinking. Design does the polish. Neither needs the panel.

SAY: Now the part nobody knows — two backups that work no matter what the panel is doing.
Backup one: you already downloaded the skill as a zip. Attach that exact zip right into your
Design chat with the plus button and say "use this skill for this design." Claude reads the
file directly — watch, it takes four seconds. Backup two: run the skill in a regular chat like
you already do, then bring what it produced into Design and polish it there. The skill's job is
the thinking; Design's job is the looks. Neither one needs that panel. You are never, ever
stuck.
