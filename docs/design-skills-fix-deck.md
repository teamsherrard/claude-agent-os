## Slide 2 — How it actually works
HEADLINE: One Account. One Skill Drawer.
BULLETS: Your Claude account has ONE skill list — the "drawer" · It lives at claude.ai/customize/skills · Claude Design shows whatever is in that drawer
SUB: Design can't see it = it's not in the drawer Design is looking at.

## Slide 3 — Check #1
HEADLINE: Same Account. Both Places.
BULLETS: Look at the account initials/email in the corner of Design · Now the corner of your regular Claude · They must be the EXACT same account
SUB: Skills never transfer between accounts. Team + personal = two separate drawers.

A skill uploaded on one will never show on the other. Signed into the wrong one? Switch, and you might be done right here.
