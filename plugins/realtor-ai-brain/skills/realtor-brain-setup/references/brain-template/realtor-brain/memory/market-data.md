*memory · the researched local market numbers, one dated block per month — written by the Market System*

> writes them into this file; the report, the YouTube script, the shorts, the infographic, and the
> distribution pack all quote this block and never re-search. That's what keeps every piece in a month
> stating identical figures.
>

> it covers — nothing here is ever estimated, interpolated, or forecast.
