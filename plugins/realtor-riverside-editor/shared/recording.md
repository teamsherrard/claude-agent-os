# Recording — where quality is actually won

The best edit cannot rescue a bad recording, and the cheapest minute in the whole workflow is the one before the agent presses record. This craft coaches that minute. It records nothing itself.

## Why record straight into Riverside

Nothing to upload, the transcript is ready the moment they stop, each person is captured locally at full quality, and the file is already where the Studio edits and publishes. Camera files work too (upload on the project page, big 4K files included), but there is a wait while it uploads and processes.

## The 60-second check (say it as a checklist, one line each)

1. **Frame** — eyes on the top third line, a hand's width of space above the head, camera at eye level (a laptop on books counts). Sit slightly off-centre if you want room for on-screen text.
2. **Light** — face a window or a lamp; never sit with the window behind you. One light in front beats three behind.
3. **Sound** — the mic is the upgrade that matters most. A $30 lav or a USB mic a fist away from the mouth. Close the door; turn off the fan.
4. **Background** — tidy, a bit of depth, nothing brighter than your face.
5. **Phone** — do not disturb on, notifications off.
6. **Energy** — stand or sit tall, first line memorised, smile before you start.

## Set up the session

- Pull the script or outline if one exists (YouTube System `youtube-script`, Short-Form System scripts). Offer Riverside's teleprompter for it.
- Settings worth confirming once: record at the highest quality the plan allows, 16:9 for YouTube, separate tracks on for interviews.
- For a guest: send the invite link, tell them to use Chrome on a computer with headphones, and do a 10-second sound check before the real take.

## Habits that make the edit better (tell them once, not every time)

- **Open on the hook.** Say the first line as if the video starts there, because it will.
- **Flub it? Pause, then say the whole sentence again.** A clean pause makes the cut invisible. Never restart mid-sentence.
- **Clap or say "cut that"** after a bad take; it shows up in the transcript and the Studio finds it instantly.
- **One idea per breath.** Short sentences cut cleanly into reels.
- **End on the call-to-action, then hold still for two seconds.** Clean endings need a clean tail.
- **Planning reels from this?** Say each key point as a complete thought that makes sense alone.

## After they stop

Say: *"Got it. Give Riverside a couple of minutes to finish processing, then say 'edit my video.'"* Check `platform_list_recordings`: a status of `uploading` or processing means wait.
