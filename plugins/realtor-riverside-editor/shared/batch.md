# Weekly batch — one recording in, a week of content out

The cohort cadence is two long-forms and several reels a week. This runs the whole line from one sentence: *"Batch this week's video."*

## What one batch produces

1. The **long-form** edit (the long-form recipe, `studio-longform`).
2. **3 reels** cut from it (default 3, up to 5 — `repurpose.md`).
3. Optional: the **podcast** audio (`podcast.md`).
4. A **publish plan**: the long-form to YouTube, the reels spread across the week on the slots the Short-Form System gives.

## Order of work (so a stop never wastes anything)

1. Creative direction + transcript read, ONCE, for the whole batch. One paragraph, one yes that covers the long-form cuts and the reel picks.
2. Long-form to a review draft. Log it.
3. Reels, one at a time, each to done. Log each.
4. Section map to the YouTube System; hook lines to the Short-Form System. Wait for their packages before any publish (`boundaries.md`).
5. ONE delivery message: the long-form + the N reels, each labelled, each with its link and length, then the 2 to 3 things only they can judge.
6. Publish only on their word, one summary covering every post, one route per post.

## Time to quote

A 12-minute long-form plus 3 reels: about 90 minutes of Studio work including renders, no credits. Say *"I'll have the whole week ready in about an hour and a half."*

## Resume

Everything is in one checkpoint log (the source edit's). "Finish my batch" reads it and runs only what never landed.
