# Friday Scorecard — what's slipping

The daily wrap closes a day; this closes the week. On demand (most agents say it Friday
afternoon), read the week and answer in **one screen, ~15 lines max**:

1. **THE WEEK** — one line: appointments held by type (showings · consults · open houses),
   promises marked Done vs rolled.
2. **KEPT** — 2–3 lines max of what actually got done. Honest, not a pep talk.
3. **SLIPPING** — the follow-ups overdue or rolled more than once, each with who · what ·
   days late. **Every slipped item comes with its fix:** "say 'draft them' and the catch-up
   emails are in your Gmail." Include the oldest unanswered items from the waiting-on scan
   (who owes YOU) in one line.
4. **GONE QUIET** — up to 3 active clients with no contact past the follow-up cadence in
   `operations.md`, each with a one-line re-touch suggestion drawn from their block ("Lees —
   promised comps two weeks ago").
5. **NEXT WEEK** — Monday's first appointment (with its prep line) + the top 3 money-making
   actions, drawn from the ledger, not invented.

Rules: read `memory/deadlines.md`, `memory/clients.md`, and the week's calendar — never guess
at what happened. No shame framing; the scorecard exists so Monday starts aimed, not so Friday
ends guilty. If the agent says **"draft them"**, write the catch-up drafts for every SLIPPING
and GONE QUIET item in their voice, report the count, log the touches, sync.

**Boundaries:** productivity only. Pipeline value, GCI pace, and lead-source analytics are a
future product, not this skill; content performance belongs to the YouTube and Short-Form
systems' own reviews.
