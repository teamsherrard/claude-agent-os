# The Intake — 5 questions, and how to pre-answer every one of them

Only for the **relocation guide** (the locked first campaign). The Brain already knows their city,
communities, price bands, voice, offer, readers, and proof — **never re-ask any of that.** These five are the
relocation-specific facts the Brain genuinely doesn't hold, and they're the difference between a guide that
could be about anywhere and one only *this* agent could have written (house rules #8). Both entry paths
run this the same way — the navigator (Step 4) and the magnet skill when it's entered directly.

## How to run it

- **Before Q1, check for a saved intake.** Silently read `identity/market.md` for a `## Relocation intake`
  block. If it's there (a wiped session, a second sitting), **don't re-ask** — confirm the lot in one line
  (*"I've still got your answers from last time — [feeders], [communities], the [question] question. Still
  right?"*) and move on. A yes or silence = use them.
- **One question at a time.** Never stack them, never paste all five, never send a form.
- **Pre-answer every one from the Brain first**, then ask them to confirm — so the realtor's job is to nod,
  not to think. *"I've got X — right?"* beats *"What's your X?"* every time. All five have a pre-fill below;
  none is asked open-ended.
- **If they're unsure, say "you pick," or go quiet → use your pre-filled answer and move on.** Never stall
  on an intake question (house rules #9).
- **Five is the cap.** Anything else the guide needs comes from the Brain, a quick sourced check, or is left out.
- **Plain words only** (house rules #1): the agent never hears "feeder market," "intake," "avatar," or a file name.

## Q1 — Where are people moving FROM?

*The single most valuable answer here. It sets up the comparisons that make the guide feel written for the
reader — what your money gets you there vs. here, commute, cost of living, weather.*

- **Pre-fill from:** `market.md` → "Why people move TO this market" and "Common misconceptions."
- **Ask like:** *"Most people moving to [City] — are they coming from [Place A] and [Place B]? Anywhere else
  I should speak to?"*
- **If unknown:** name the two or three biggest realistic sources for their market (the nearby higher-priced
  metro, the main out-of-province / out-of-state source, out-of-country if it applies) and ask them to confirm.
- **Default if they don't know:** the largest nearby higher-priced metro + one out-of-province / out-of-state source.
- **Fair Housing guardrail (house rules #5):** answers are **places only** — a city, a province/state, a
  country. If the agent answers with a nationality, ethnicity, language group, immigration status, or family
  type, translate it to the place without comment (*"people from Ontario"* → Ontario) and never carry the
  original wording into the guide or the page.

## Q2 — Which communities do you want relocation leads in?

*They may serve fifteen. The guide should feature the three to five they actually want to be known for —
and the funnel's Local Market section uses the SAME list (house rules #7).*

- **Pre-fill from:** `market.md` → "Communities / neighbourhoods served" + "Top neighbourhoods buyers ask
  about" + the "underrated / up-and-coming" one.
- **Ask like:** *"I'll build the neighbourhood section around [A], [B], [C] and [D] — those the right ones to
  be known for?"*
- **Default:** the top 4 in `market.md`, with the up-and-coming one included (it makes the guide feel insider).

## Q3 — What's the #1 thing people moving there always ask you?

*This becomes the guide's sharpest page and often its hook. It's the agent's real-world experience — the part
no chatbot has.*

- **Pre-fill from:** `market.md` → "What buyers consistently get wrong here" + "Common misconceptions" +
  `avatars.md` → the core fear (if a relocation reader is in there).
- **Ask like:** *"When someone's moving to [City], what's the question you get every single time? I'd guess
  it's [guess] — close?"*
- **Default:** the biggest misconception in `market.md`, framed as a question.

## Q4 — Have you helped anyone relocate there? (one line is plenty)

*One real relocation story is worth more than a page of claims — it goes in the guide's "what good looks
like" page and in the funnel's Proof section.*

- **Pre-fill from:** `proof.md` (and `identity/story-bank.md` if it exists) — if there's already a relocation
  client or story in there, just confirm it: *"I'll use the [feeder]-to-[City] story about [client] — right?"*
- **Ask like (nothing in the Brain):** *"Got a relocation client I can mention — someone who moved in from out
  of town and how it went? A sentence is plenty. If not, no problem — we'll lean on your local depth."*
- **If they have none:** completely fine, say so lightly and move on. **Never invent one** (house rules #5, #8).

## Q5 — What does everyone get wrong about living there?

*The spice. The honest, slightly contrarian, experience-based detail that makes the guide feel real instead
of promotional — and the thing competing guides won't say.*

- **Pre-fill from:** `market.md` → "What this agent knows that most agents don't" + "Why people leave" +
  "Common misconceptions."
- **Ask like:** *"Last one — what surprises people once they actually live in [City]? From what you've told
  me I'd guess it's [pre-fill] — close? Even if it's not flattering, that's the bit people remember."*
- **Default:** the "misconceptions" line from `market.md`.
- **Keep it Fair-Housing safe:** verifiable facts about the place — commute, cost, weather, timelines,
  amenities, schools by name — never anything about who lives where or who "belongs" somewhere
  (house rules #5).

## Write back what's NEW, silently, before handing off

House rules' write-back law. Compare each answer to its pre-fill and append **only what the Brain didn't
already hold** — a nod to the pre-fill writes nothing (no duplicate lines). Write one block to
`identity/market.md` (create it if absent, update it if present):

```
## Relocation intake
- Feeder places: [Q1]
- Relocation focus communities: [Q2]
- The #1 relocation question: [Q3]
- Relocation story: [Q4, or "none yet"]
- What people get wrong: [Q5]
```

Then **realtor-brain-sync PUSH** (write → push → verify). Never narrate this or name the file (house rules #1).

## What you hand to the magnet skill

A short, plain bundle — the locked focus plus the five answers:

```
FOCUS (locked): Relocation guide — "Moving to [City]? Start Here."   (title on the metro people search — house rules #11)
Q1 feeder places:         [PLACES only — city / province-state / country]
Q2 communities:           [3–5 named — the funnel uses this same list]
Q3 the #1 question:       [their real one]
Q4 relocation proof:      [one line, or "none — use local depth"]
Q5 what people get wrong: [the honest detail]
```

Everything else — voice, offer, price bands, credentials, compliance — the magnet skill reads from the Brain
itself. Don't collect it here.
