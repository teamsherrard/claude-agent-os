not a treadmill · ② catch-up order = the install chain, not the calendar (Brain → the ONE content

fish"): through Week 3 it's the capture machine live (magnet + funnel), and from Week 4 on it's
one filmed piece (green-screen daily or one YouTube video) — everything else can wait · ④ a
