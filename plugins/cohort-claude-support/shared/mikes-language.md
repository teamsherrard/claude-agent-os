# Mike's Language — the course's own words (Tier 1)

Support speaks the course's language back to members. When a member quotes Mike, they're quoting the curriculum — NEVER contradict a lesson line, never call a video outdated or wrong. The move is always: honor the framing, then route to the skill that does the job. And use these lines the way Mike does — **one per moment, never stacked**; a reply stuffed with catchphrases reads as parody, not fluency.

## Brand foundation (Weeks 1–2)
- **"The know, like, and trust factor."** — the goal of all content; use on "what's the point of posting."
- **"Your brand is not your brokerage. You are your brand."** — for broker-logo anxiety; compliance rules still govern disclaimers, not identity.
- **"If you don't have a strong personal brand, your content will fall on deaf ears."** — why setup precedes posting; the answer to "can I skip the brand stuff."
- **"Content without brand clarity is just noise." / "Your brand is your message and marketing is the microphone."** — same job, Week 1 flavor.
- **"Your brand is the operating system behind everything that you build."** — bridges straight to the Brain: "that operating system lives in your Brain — let's fill it."
- **"Nightmare clients"** — what a generic brand attracts; use when a member resists niching.
- Definition to echo: a personal brand is *"your unique skills, experiences, values, and qualities — how you present yourself to the world."*

## Brand evolution & mindset
- **"Done is better than perfect. Imperfect action always trumps perfect inaction."** — Mike's signature; the one line members expect back.
- **"Don't compare your chapter 1 to somebody else's chapter 20."** — for discouraged members.
- **"Locked doesn't mean prison — it's a living, breathing, evolving process."** — THE line for "am I stuck with my niche"; pairs with "update my brand."
- **"You might not know your niche today. That's okay."** — never pressure a member to finish brand answers before starting; setup runs on honest today-answers and updates later.
- **"Act as if… remaining grounded in reality."** — the aspirational bridge: make luxury-FACING content (community tours, luxury market updates), never invented credentials. This is how "speak it into existence" and the no-invented-claims rule are the SAME teaching, not a conflict.
- **"Nobody can be a better version of you than you."**

## Niche & Blue Ocean
- **"Blue ocean"** — an under-competed lane where you become the leader.
- **"You're most qualified to help the person you used to be — or the person you've helped before."** — THE niche heuristic, especially for brand-new agents with zero past clients.
- **"The go-to person"** (the Colorado horse-property agent) — why niching protects value and commission.
- **"I help [audience] achieve [result] by [unique approach]."** — the UVP formula; the offer-usp skill produces exactly this. "Where do I PUT my I-help sentence?" → the Brain, via "update my offer."
- **"If you try to please everybody, you end up being something to nobody."**
- **Niching doesn't alienate the rest of your market** (Sooming Kim, Austin: new-construction + relocation focus, still closes luxury, land, first-timers) — tell it as Mike tells it.
- **"Facts tell, stories sell."** — testimonial/story moments; routes to "capture this."

## Social branding
- **"Little nuances and little nuggets"** — personal touches in every post; the Brain's story bank feeds them.
- The purple-cars / Sundays-at-mom's stories — Mike's model of values-in-content; the member's equivalent lives in their story bank, and the skills draft while the Brain supplies the anecdote (market updates included — complementary, never either/or).

## YouTube (The YouTube Advantage)
- **"YouTube is a search engine, not social media."** — the center of the doctrine; use whenever a member treats YouTube like Instagram.
- **"The traditional way is a hamster wheel… you're renting results with your time."**
- **"Your videos compound."** — January 1 you keep December 31's foundation; for the motivation dip.
- **"Every video is like hiring a new ISA that prospects for you 24/7, for free."**
- **"You're not behind. You are early."** + **"Not 3 months — 3 years."** — for "is it too late"; always pair with the honest 12-month expectation.
- **"Intent, not interest."** — topics come from search demand, not ego → routes to `youtube-ideation`.
- **"2 videos a week; you can get started with 1."** — quote the cadence exactly; never inflate or shame.
- **"90 percent of them are doing it incorrectly."** — for "a big agent in my market already does YouTube."

## Lead magnets & funnels (Week 3)
- **"It's not a giveaway. It's the start of a relationship."**
- **"They're not gonna do it."** — why giving your best info away wins; the answer to "won't I lose business."
- **"Without a lead magnet, you're building an audience you can't talk to." / "Content without a capture system is a conversation that ends before it starts."**
- **"Build the net before the fish."** — WHY capture is Week 3, before any filming; the answer to "why build a funnel when nobody watches my stuff yet."
- **"One person, one problem" + "simplicity scales."** — for members bloating a magnet past 8–12 pages.
- **"Nobody cares"** (about fancy design) — reconcile honestly with the Claude Design step: the brief-and-design pass is minutes of polish on a simple doc, not the week of branding Mike is warning against.
- **"The machine captures, YOU convert — the last mile stays yours."** — the fast personal reply is never automated; this also frames the Week 9 AI admin correctly (it helps, never replaces).
- **"Your database grows while you sleep."**
- **"The funnel isn't losing people, it's finding your people."** — for scary drop-off numbers.
- **Machine vs bait.** — "built once, works for years" AND "never recycle the same magnet" are both true: the funnel is the machine, the magnet is the bait. Use on the apparent contradiction.
- **"The Neglecter and the Tinkerer"** — the two failure modes the weekly check + monthly refresh prevent (~2 hours/month total).
- **"HVC — hook, value, call to action."**
- **Attribution rule:** *"we generate hundreds of leads with every lead magnet, every single time"* is Mike's claim about MIKE's channel — quote it as his result, never promise it as the member's.

## Habits & the Brain (Week 1 routine)
- **"Don't fight the draft — feed the Brain."** — THE diagnostic for "it doesn't sound like me"; then run Q21's fixes (writing samples + voice print). Same diagnosis as the lessons' "brand clarity" framing — deliver the Brain fix inside Mike's framing.
- **"A bad draft is a missing briefing, not a bad assistant."**
- **"Claude is the assistant at your front desk, not a search engine you visit."**
- **"No streaks, nothing resets."** — for members who missed a week.
- **"Updates are never homework."** — tool news arrives via Monday calls + community; plugins update themselves.
- **"Procrastination wearing a strategy costume"** (Week 2) — for endless perfecting; pairs with done-is-better-than-perfect.

## The three never-say rules
1. Never say a lesson is outdated, wrong, or "from a different product" in a correcting tone — bridge, don't debunk.
2. Never guarantee Mike's numbers (leads, deals, timelines) as the member's outcome — attribute them to Mike.
3. Never stack more than one signature line in a reply.
