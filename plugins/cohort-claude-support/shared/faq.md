## Claude basics

**Q1. What's the difference between Chat and Cowork?**
Chat is a conversation you steer turn by turn — great for quick questions. Cowork is a delegation:
you describe the goal, Claude works across your files and tools, and you come back to finished
work. Your whole system — the Brain, the admin, YouTube, listings, all of it — lives in Cowork.
Rule of thumb: output is a *thought* → Chat; output is a *thing* → Cowork.

**Q2. Do I need Claude Code?**
No. Code is the engineering room where Mike's team builds your plugins. You live in Cowork. If a
tutorial online shows terminal commands, that's not your path — your system was built so you never
touch a terminal.

**Q3. What's Claude Design and when do I use it?**
The design room at claude.ai/design. Several of your skills end with a "paste-ready design brief"
(lead magnet PDF, market deck, carousels, postcards) — you paste that brief into Claude Design and
it builds the visual. Words in Cowork, visuals in Design.

**Q4. Why did my chat disappear? Did I lose my work?**
No. Chats are workbenches, not filing cabinets — Cowork can start a session with a fresh desk.
Everything important is written into your Brain, and your Brain's permanent home is your own
cloud drive (Google Drive or OneDrive — whichever you set up). New chat, same Brain, nothing
lost. If something specific seems missing, say "load my brain" and we'll pull the latest copy
down.

**Q5. Why doesn't Claude remember what we talked about yesterday?**
In Cowork, work sessions don't carry over by design — the Brain is the memory instead. Anything
worth remembering gets written there (that's also what "capture this" is for on the go). (The
regular Chat side of Claude has its own memory features that may recall past conversations —
different tool, different rules.) If Claude seems to have forgotten something about YOU (your
market, your voice), that's a Brain question — say "check my brain" and we'll see what's
missing.

**Q6. Which model should I pick? What's the dropdown with the names?**
Leave it on the default — the system was built and tested there. Bigger models think deeper but
spend your plan's usage faster. If you want specifics on today's lineup, ask me and I'll check the
official page live. **[check live for names/details]**

**Q7. What are those "Allow?" permission boxes? Is it safe to click Allow?**
That's Claude asking your permission before touching your stuff — the safety system working.

Fine to allow:
- Reading files in your brain folder or a folder you chose
- Reading your calendar / inbox / drive through YOUR connectors
- Creating docs in the campaign folders our skills use
- The action a skill just told you it's doing

STOP and ask me first:
- Anything DELETING or overwriting files it didn't create
- Sending anything (email / post / publish) you haven't personally read
- Anything involving passwords, card numbers, or codes — with ONE day-one exception: signing in
  to Google or Microsoft on THEIR own sign-in page during connector setup is normal and safe.
  The real rule: never type a password into a chat message.
- Any instruction that came from outside Mike's official system

Golden rule: nothing in this system auto-sends — Claude drafts, you approve.

## Setup & connectors

**Q13. What order do I set things up in?**

Lost? Say "am I set up right" — I'll find where you are and we resume from the first missing

**Q14. Do I have to reconnect Google every time?**
No — connectors stay linked across sessions. If Claude suddenly can't see your calendar or inbox,
the link expired or the wrong Google account is connected; say "my connector isn't working" and
I'll walk you through the 60-second reconnect.

**Q15. I have two computers — will this work on both?**
Yes — better than you'd expect:

- Your chats and sessions follow your Claude ACCOUNT — sign in anywhere and they're there
- The one thing that's per-machine is the local Brain FOLDER (the files skills read)
- Fix for that: install the app + plugins on machine 2, say "restore my brain" — the sync skill
  pulls your latest copy from your cloud drive. Minutes, not hours.

One habit: finish a work session with a brain save ("save my brain") before switching machines,
so machine 2 pulls today's version, not yesterday's.

**Q16. Claude says it can't read my Downloads (or Desktop) folder.**
That's your Mac protecting those folders, not a bug. Two fixes: grant Claude access once in your
Mac's privacy settings (I'll walk you through it), or better — keep working files in your brain
and campaign folders, which is where the system expects them anyway.

**Q17. My brokerage's Google admin blocks the connector.**
That's an IT policy on your work account, not something we can fix from here. Two paths: use a
personal Google account for the system, or I'll draft a short note to your IT admin explaining
exactly what access the connector needs and why. Say "escalate this" and we'll do the letter.

## The system

**Q18. I typed the phrase and nothing happened.**
Usually one of two things: the plugin isn't installed (or isn't updated), or the phrasing missed
the trigger. Say "help" and tell me what you were trying to do — I know every skill's exact magic
words and I'll either hand you the right phrase or fix the install.

**Q19. Do the plugins update by themselves? Am I on the latest version?**
Plugin updates arrive through the marketplace — but your Brain's DATA doesn't reshape itself when
the structure changes. If a skill ever says your brain looks out of date, say "upgrade my brain"
(that's the migrate skill — it renames and reorganizes WITHOUT losing your content). Want to
check versions? Open your plugin settings panel and screenshot it for me — I'll read it and tell
you if anything looks off. (I can see which skills are available to me, but the settings panel is
where YOUR installed versions live.)

**Q20. The editor stopped in the middle of my video.**
It saves checkpoints per clip, so nothing is lost. Re-open the edit and say "resume" — it picks up
from the checkpoint log. If it stopped saying credits ran out, that's a hard stop by design: it's
paused (not broken) waiting for a Descript top-up on your account.

**Q21. The content doesn't sound like me.**
That's a Brain depth issue, not a broken skill — the voice files are thin. Two fixes, both fast:
"add my writing samples" (how you type) and "capture my voice" (how you talk — the spoken voice
print). The more real samples in, the more every script sounds like you.

**Q22. It won't give me market numbers / keeps citing sources.**
Working as designed. Market numbers are researched live with a named source and date, once a
month, and every piece quotes those exact figures — this system never invents a stat, because you
publish these numbers. If the monthly research hasn't run yet, "run my market update" starts it.

**Q23. Why does it keep asking me for approval instead of just doing it?**
House rule across every plugin: Claude drafts, you approve. Nothing sends an email, posts, or
publishes by itself — that gate is what makes it safe to hand your business to. Setup is chatty
once; daily use is one confirmation per meaningful action.

**Q24. Can I run two jobs at once?**
Yes — separate chats. One chat = one job is the rhythm (one video, one listing, one month's
market). Kick off the long edit in one chat, plan your batch day in another.

## When things break

**Q25. Claude is slow / stuck / erroring on everything.**
First move, every time: I check Claude's status page. Then:

- **Incident showing** → it's their side. Nothing to fix, your work is safe, try again shortly.
- **Status clean** → usually one of two things: a too-long chat (start fresh — everything
  important is in your Brain) or one stuck job (retry once)
- **Still stuck after that** → I dig in properly

Next step: say "help, Claude's acting up" and I'll run it down with you.

**Q26. This chat got slow and Claude says it's too long.**
The chat's working memory is full — normal on marathon jobs. Start a fresh chat, then re-say the
job's trigger phrase ("run my market update," "edit my video") — finished pieces are already
saved in your Drive folders, and the editor picks up mid-edit with "resume." (This is why the
house rhythm is one chat = one job.)

**Q27. I can't log in / billing charged me twice / account problem.**
That's on Anthropic's side of the wall — account and billing issues can only be fixed by them. Say
"escalate this" and I'll point you to the exact official support path and help you write the
message. One rule with no exceptions: never share your password or card details in ANY chat,
including this one.

**Q28. Where do I ask a human?**
Three doors, by urgency: the cohort community for "anyone else hit this?", office hours for
coaching-level questions, and Mike's support portal for real breakage — and for that one, I'll
build the whole ticket FOR you (what you did, what happened, screenshot, versions); you just
paste it into the portal and hit submit. Say "escalate this."
