## Claude basics

**Q1. What's the difference between Chat and Cowork?**
Rule of thumb: output is a *thought* → Chat; output is a *thing* → Cowork.

- **Chat** — a conversation you steer turn by turn; great for quick questions
- **Cowork** — a delegation: you describe the goal, Claude works across your files and tools, and
  you come back to finished work

Your whole system — the Brain, the admin, YouTube, listings, all of it — lives in Cowork.

**Q2. Do I need Claude Code?**
No. Code is the engineering room where Mike's team builds your plugins. You live in Cowork. If a
tutorial online shows terminal commands, that's not your path — your system was built so you never
touch a terminal.

**Q3. What's Claude Design and when do I use it?**
The design room at claude.ai/design. Several of your skills end with a "paste-ready design brief"
(lead magnet PDF, market deck, carousels, postcards) — you paste that brief into Claude Design and
it builds the visual. Words in Cowork, visuals in Design.

**Q4. Why did my chat disappear? Did I lose my work?**
No. Chats are workbenches, not filing cabinets — Cowork can start a session with a fresh desk.
Everything important is written into your Brain, and your Brain's permanent home is your own
cloud drive (Google Drive or OneDrive — whichever you set up). New chat, same Brain, nothing
lost. If something specific seems missing, say "load my brain" and we'll pull the latest copy
down.

**Q5. Why doesn't Claude remember what we talked about yesterday?**
In Cowork, work sessions don't carry over by design — the Brain is the memory instead. Anything
worth remembering gets written there (that's also what "capture this" is for on the go). (The
regular Chat side of Claude has its own memory features that may recall past conversations —
different tool, different rules.) If Claude seems to have forgotten something about YOU (your
market, your voice), that's a Brain question — say "check my brain" and we'll see what's
missing.
